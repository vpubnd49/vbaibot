# Mau cau truc van ban hanh chinh (Tinh Lam Dong)

Tai lieu nay chua cau truc CHUAN cho cac loai VBHC thuong gap, rut ra tu
cac van ban THUC TE cua UBND tinh Lam Dong (thu muc `bosung/`).

## 1. QUYET DINH (QD) – Ban hanh quy dinh chuc nang nhiem vu

### Cau truc QD

```
HEADER: [UY BAN NHAN DAN TINH LAM DONG] | [CHXHCNVN]
SO: /2025/QD-UBND (QPPL) hoac /QD-UBND (ca biet)
DIA DANH: Lam Dong, ngay ... thang ... nam ...

TEN LOAI: QUYET DINH (IN HOA, DAM, co 14-15)
TRICH YEU: Ban hanh quy dinh chuc nang, nhiem vu, quyen han
           cua So [ten So] tinh Lam Dong (thuong, co 14)

THAM QUYEN: UY BAN NHAN DAN TINH LAM DONG (in hoa, dam)

CAN CU:
- Can cu Luat To chuc chinh quyen dia phuong so 72/2025/QH15;
- Can cu Nghi dinh 150/2025/ND-CP ngay 12/6/2025;
- Can cu Nghi dinh 158/2018/ND-CP ngay 22/11/2018;
- Can cu Thong tu so [xx]/2025/TT-B[xx] (Bo chuyen nganh);
- Can cu NQ 03/NQ-HDND ngay 03/7/2025 (thanh lap CQCM);
- Can cu De an 05/DA-UBND ngay 02/7/2025;
- Theo de nghi cua Giam doc So [ten So].

QUYET DINH:
Dieu 1. Ban hanh kem theo Quyet dinh nay "Quy dinh chuc nang..."
Dieu 2. Hieu luc thi hanh (thay the QD cu nao: LĐ cu, Dak Nong cu, Binh Thuan cu)
Dieu 3. To chuc thuc hien (liet ke doi tuong thi hanh)./.

NOI NHAN (QD QPPL):
- Van phong Chinh phu;
- Bo [chuyen nganh] (Vu Phap che);
- Bo Noi vu (Vu Phap che);
- Bo Tu phap (Cuc kiem tra VB QPPL);
- TT Tinh uy, TT HDND tinh;
- CT, cac PCT UBND tinh;
- Doan DBQH don vi tinh Lam Dong;
- Ban To chuc Tinh uy;
- Website Chinh phu;
- Nhu Dieu 3;
- Bao Lam Dong, Dai PTTH tinh;
- TT Ha tang va Cong nghe so tinh;
- TT Luu tru lich su;
- Luu: VT, [ma phong].

KY: TM. UY BAN NHAN DAN / CHU TICH / Ho Van Muoi
```

### Dac diem QD quan trong

- Dieu 2 LUON liet ke ro QD cu thay the (thuong 2-3 QD tu 3 tinh cu)
- Dieu 3 danh sach: Chanh VP UBND tinh, GD So Noi vu, GD So [lien quan],
  GD/Thu truong cac so, ban, nganh; CT UBND cac xa, phuong, dac khu
- Kem theo QUY DINH chi tiet (van ban rieng, co Chuong/Dieu)

## 2. KE HOACH (KH)

### Cau truc KH

```
HEADER: [UY BAN NHAN DAN TINH LAM DONG] | [CHXHCNVN]
SO: /KH-UBND
DIA DANH: Lam Dong, ngay ... thang ... nam ...

TEN LOAI: KE HOACH (IN HOA, DAM)
TRICH YEU: To chuc thuc hien [ten VB chi dao]

MO DAU: Thuc hien [VB/Chi thi/NQ nao], UBND tinh ban hanh KH..., nhu sau:

I. MUC DICH, YEU CAU
  1. Muc dich
  2. Yeu cau

II. CAC NHIEM VU VA GIAI PHAP TRONG TAM
  1. [Nhom nhiem vu 1]
     - Noi dung cu the
     - Co quan chu tri: So [ten]
     - Co quan phoi hop: [danh sach]
     - Thoi gian hoan thanh: Truoc ngay DD/MM/YYYY
  2. [Nhom nhiem vu 2]
     ...

III. TO CHUC THUC HIEN
  1. So Noi vu
  2. So Khoa hoc va Cong nghe
  3. So Tai chinh
  4. UBND cac xa, phuong, dac khu

KET THUC: "Trong qua trinh trien khai..., phan anh ve UBND tinh
(qua So ...) de tong hop, huong dan giai quyet./."

NOI NHAN + KY: CHU TICH / Ho Van Muoi
```

### Quy tac KH bat buoc

- Moi nhiem vu PHAI CO 3 yeu to: **Co quan chu tri**, **Co quan phoi hop**, **Thoi gian hoan thanh**
- Phan III phan RO trach nhiem tung So, nganh
- Ket thuc bang dau `./.`

## 3. THONG BAO KET LUAN (TB)

### Cau truc TB

```
HEADER: [UY BAN NHAN DAN TINH LAM DONG] | [CHXHCNVN]
SO: /TB-UBND
DIA DANH: Lam Dong, ngay ... thang ... nam ...

TEN LOAI: THONG BAO (IN HOA, DAM)
TRICH YEU: Ket luan, chi dao cua dong chi [Ho ten],
[Chuc danh day du] tai buoi lam viec voi [doi tuong]
ve [noi dung]

MO DAU: Ngay [DD/MM/YYYY], dong chi [Ho ten, chuc danh] da chu tri
buoi lam viec. Tham du: [danh sach lanh dao]. Sau khi nghe bao cao...,
y kien..., [ten lanh dao] ket luan va chi dao nhu sau:

1. Danh gia chung
2. Nhiem vu trong thoi gian toi
  2.1. Yeu cau [doi tuong cu the]
    a) Noi dung 1
    b) Noi dung 2
  2.2. Giao [So/nganh] [nhiem vu cu the]
  2.3. Giao [So/nganh khac]
3. Giao Van phong UBND tinh tham muu...
4. De nghi [Bi thu dang uy...] quan tam lanh dao...

KET: "Yeu cau Thu truong cac co quan, don vi... nghiem tuc
trien khai thuc hien./."

NOI NHAN:
- TT Tinh uy (de bao cao);
- TT HDND tinh (de bao cao);
- TT Dang uy UBND tinh (de bao cao);
- CT, cac PCT UBND tinh;
- Cac so, ban, nganh;
- CVP, PCVP UBND tinh;
- TT Dang uy cac xa, phuong, dac khu;
- UBND cac xa, phuong, dac khu;
- Luu: VT, [ma phong] ([ten CV]).

KY: CHU TICH / Ho Van Muoi
```

### Dac diem TB ket luan

- Mo dau LUON neu boi canh cuop hop: ai chu tri, ai tham du, noi dung
- Phan chi dao dung cac menh lenh: "Yeu cau...", "Giao..."
- Noi nhan: cap tren "de bao cao", cap duoi KHONG ghi "de bao cao"

## 4. TO TRINH (TTr)

### Cau truc TTr

```
HEADER: [CO QUAN TRINH] | [CHXHCNVN]
SO: /TTr-[viet tat CQ]
DIA DANH: Lam Dong, ngay ... thang ... nam ...

TEN LOAI: TO TRINH (IN HOA, DAM)
V/v [noi dung trinh]
Kinh gui: [co quan co tham quyen quyet dinh]

I. SU CAN THIET VA CAN CU PHAP LY
  1. Su can thiet
  2. Can cu phap ly

II. NOI DUNG DE NGHI
  1. [Chi tiet noi dung trinh]
  2. [Phuong an de xuat]

III. TO CHUC THUC HIEN (neu can)

KET: "[Co quan] kinh trinh [co quan co tham quyen] xem xet, quyet dinh./."

NOI NHAN + KY: GIAM DOC (hoac tham quyen) / [Ho ten]
```

## 5. BAO CAO (BC)

### Cau truc BC

```
HEADER: [CO QUAN BAO CAO] | [CHXHCNVN]
SO: /BC-[viet tat]
DIA DANH: Lam Dong, ngay ... thang ... nam ...

TEN LOAI: BAO CAO (IN HOA, DAM)
TRICH YEU: Tinh hinh [noi dung] [ky bao cao]

I. TINH HINH THUC HIEN
  1. Ket qua dat duoc
  2. Ton tai, han che
  3. Nguyen nhan

II. PHUONG HUONG, NHIEM VU THOI GIAN TOI

III. KIEN NGHI, DE XUAT (neu co)

KET THUC: ./.

NOI NHAN + KY
```

---

## 6. DON VI HANH CHINH CAP DUOI (LUU Y QUAN TRONG)

- KHONG CON: huyen, thi xa, thanh pho truc thuoc tinh
- MOI: xa, phuong, dac khu (truc thuoc tinh - mo hinh chinh quyen 2 cap)
- BIET THUC DUNG DONG NHAT: "Uy ban nhan dan cac xa, phuong, dac khu"
  (phai co tu "cac", khong bo sot)
- KHONG CON: "UBND huyen...", "UBND thanh pho Da Lat"
  → Thay bang "UBND xa [ten], phuong [ten], dac khu [ten]"
